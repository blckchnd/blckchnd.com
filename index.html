GPH.AI

GPH.AI is a decentralized Graphene blockchain designed for markets, digital assets and blockchain applications.

GPH.AI provides the shared blockchain layer for RUDEX, GPH.BET and GOLD BUG.

GPH.BET

GPH.BET is a non-custodial on-chain trading application for taking directional positions on GPH against USD on the GPH.AI Graphene blockchain.

Positions use native GPH and SmartCoin USD, are executed and recorded directly on-chain, with profit and loss calculated in GPH.

Open GPH.BET→

GOLD BUG

GOLD BUG is a Graphene-based trading application for taking directional positions on the Gold/Silver Ratio (GSR).

Positions track the relative performance of gold versus silver rather than their price in fiat, with profit and loss measured in gold.
